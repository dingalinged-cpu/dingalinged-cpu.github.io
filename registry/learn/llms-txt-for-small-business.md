# Does llms.txt help a small business show up in AI? What the evidence says

*Updated October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** Probably not much. llms.txt is a proposed file that points AI models to your key pages, but studies of hundreds of thousands of sites found AI crawlers rarely fetch it, and Google says its AI features don't use it. A small business gets more from allowing AI crawlers, a current sitemap, and accurate listings.

## What the data says

- **Ahrefs (137,210 domains, May 2026):** about 28% published an llms.txt file, and 97% of those files received no traffic at all that month. Training crawlers fetched it more than AI search bots.
- **Optimizely (October 2026)** reviewed four studies covering more than 500 million AI bot visits; one test logged 84 llms.txt requests out of more than 62,100 AI bot visits.
- **Google** says its search products, including AI Overviews and AI Mode, don't use llms.txt. Google's John Mueller said in October 2026 that AI crawlers do fetch sitemaps and RSS feeds in his logs.
- **Semrush** notes no major AI platform has confirmed it reads or relies on llms.txt.

## What to do first

1. **robots.txt**: make sure you aren't blocking GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot or Bingbot.
2. **sitemap.xml**: keep it current, list it in robots.txt, submit it to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
3. **Plain HTML**: key facts should be in the page text, not only inside JavaScript or images.
4. **Listings and reviews**: these drive local recommendations far more than any file.

## When llms.txt is still worth it

It costs minutes to write and does no harm. Sites with documentation, APIs or many pages (like ours) publish one because coding agents and some tools do read it. For a typical local business, it is optional.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Google use llms.txt?

Google says its search products, including AI Overviews and AI Mode, don't use it.

### Should my small business add an llms.txt file?

It's optional. It does no harm, but crawler access, a current sitemap and accurate listings matter much more for being recommended.

### Which AI crawlers should I allow in robots.txt?

Common ones include GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User (OpenAI), ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User (Anthropic), PerplexityBot, and Bingbot, which feeds Copilot.

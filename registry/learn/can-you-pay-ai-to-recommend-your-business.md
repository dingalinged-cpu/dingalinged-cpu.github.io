# Can you pay ChatGPT (or any AI) to recommend your business?

*By Bryce Hoemann · Published October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** No. You can buy ads in ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, but they are labeled as sponsored and shown apart from the answer. OpenAI says ads do not influence what ChatGPT recommends. The businesses an assistant names come from public information it trusts, so anyone selling a guaranteed AI recommendation is overpromising.

## Ads versus answers

When you ask ChatGPT for "a good roofer in Wichita," two things can appear:

- **The answer**: a short list of businesses the assistant chose. OpenAI's ad principles say answers are "optimized based on what's most helpful to you" and that ads "do not influence the answers."
- **An ad**: a separate, labeled "Sponsored" unit. Advertisers can buy these through OpenAI's Ads Manager.

Microsoft Copilot follows a similar pattern with ads from Microsoft Advertising. Google began testing ads in AI Mode in October 2026.

## What you can pay for

- **Ads** on the assistants that sell them (ChatGPT, Copilot; Google is testing ads in AI Mode).
- **Help with the groundwork**: listings management, review requests, website copy, technical fixes. These can raise your odds. They cannot guarantee an outcome.
- **Labeled sponsored spots on directories and registries.** Company Registry has not opened paid spots yet. When it does, they will be labeled Sponsored and kept apart from the ranked results, and they will not change your rank or anyone's score.

## Red flags

Be careful with anyone who:

- **Guarantees** you will be "the #1 answer in ChatGPT." Results vary between runs. BrightLocal found only 20% to 33% of businesses reappeared when the same prompt was rerun.
- Says they have a **partnership that lets them insert you** into ChatGPT's answers. OpenAI says ads do not influence the answers, and that its own ads system controls all delivery decisions even when agencies buy ads.
- Will not tell you **what they will actually change** (listings, reviews, pages, data).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do ChatGPT ads change ChatGPT's answers?

OpenAI says they do not. Ads are matched to the conversation topic, labeled as sponsored and shown separately from the answer.

### Can an agency guarantee my business shows up in ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee it. AI answers vary from run to run and draw on many sources. An honest provider will tell you which signals they will improve, not promise a position.

### Is paying for AI SEO worth it?

It can be if the work is concrete: fixing listings, collecting reviews, rewriting pages to answer customer questions. Much of it you can do yourself for free in a few hours.

### Does a paid spot on Company Registry change my ranking?

No. Sponsored spots are not open yet. When they open, they will be labeled and shown separately, and the ranked results will be computed the same way for every business, paying or not.

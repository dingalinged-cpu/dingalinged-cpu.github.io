# Where to list your business so AI can find it: Google, Bing, Apple, directories and agent registries compared

*By Bryce Hoemann · Published October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** Start with the listings assistants already read: Google Business Profile for Google's AI, Bing Places for Microsoft Copilot, and Apple Business Connect for Apple Maps. Then cover the major directories in your category, and add agent-readable registries that publish structured data. All are free to claim; consistency across them matters most.

## Comparison

| Where | Free to claim? | Who reads it | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Yes | Google Search, Maps, AI Overviews, AI Mode | Everything local on Google |
| Bing Places for Business | Yes | Bing, Bing Maps, Microsoft Copilot | Copilot and Bing-powered search |
| Apple Business Connect | Yes | Apple Maps, Siri | iPhone users |
| Yelp, Facebook, and category directories | Usually | Frequently cited by ChatGPT and Google's AI (BrightLocal) | Reviews and category trust |
| Agent-readable registries such as Company Registry | Free listing | Any AI agent or crawler | Clean, structured facts agents can parse |

BrightLocal's study of 1.9 million local AI citations found Google Business Profile made up 28.6% of all citations and Google used it for virtually every location; Yelp was second overall and first for ChatGPT. Facebook, Tripadvisor, MapQuest, BBB and Angi also appeared often, and vertical directories mattered by industry. Copilot grounds its answers in Bing search.

## What makes Company Registry different

- **Built for machines first.** Each business is published as HTML with schema.org data, a markdown copy and JSON. Keyword answers like "best dentists in Des Moines, IA" use one fixed table format.
- **Sources are labeled.** Every record names where its facts came from. When owner claiming opens, facts stated by the owner will be shown beside registry facts and labeled, never mixed.
- **Ranking is not for sale.** Sponsored spots, when they open, will be labeled and kept apart from the ranked results.
- **Free owner claiming (coming soon).** Owners will be able to add services and details they want agents to know.

## What it is not

Company Registry is not a replacement for Google Business Profile or Bing Places. Claim those first. We are one more consistent, structured source that agents can read and cross-check.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there one place to list my business for every AI?

No. Each assistant leans on different sources. Google Business Profile, Bing Places and Apple Business Connect cover the big three ecosystems, and directories and registries add confirmation.

### Does listing my business in a registry guarantee AI will recommend it?

No. It gives agents another consistent, structured source. Recommendations still depend on reviews, relevance, location and how well your facts match across the web.

### Is my business already in Company Registry?

It may be. We seed listings from public sources such as OpenStreetMap. Open the ranked list for your service and city, for example https://registry-edge.dingalinged.workers.dev/k/dentist-minneapolis-mn/. Claiming a profile to add and correct details is coming soon.

### Will claiming a profile cost anything?

No. Claiming is not open yet; when it opens it will be free and will not affect your ranking.

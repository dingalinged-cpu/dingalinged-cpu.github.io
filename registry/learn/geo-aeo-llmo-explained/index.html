GEO, AEO, LLMO and AI SEO: what the terms mean for a local business

By Bryce Hoemann · Published October 18, 2026

Short answer: GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO (answer engine optimization), LLMO (LLM optimization) and AI SEO are overlapping names for one goal: getting your business named and cited in AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and Google's AI results. The groundwork is ordinary SEO plus consistent facts across many sources.

Definitions

Term Meaning Note
GEO, generative engine optimization Improving how often and how accurately AI-generated answers mention and cite you Ahrefs estimates about 12,000 US searches a month, up 161% year over year
AEO, answer engine optimization Structuring content so answer engines (and voice assistants) can quote it directly Older term, now used for AI answers too
LLMO, LLM optimization Same idea, named after large language models Less common
AI SEO Either GEO, or using AI tools to do SEO Ambiguous: check which one a vendor means
AI visibility How often you appear in AI answers, as a metric Tool category

How it differs from SEO

  • Short lists. A search page shows ten links; an AI answer often names two to four businesses.
  • Mentions count, not just links. Ahrefs found branded web mentions correlated more strongly with AI Overview visibility than backlinks.
  • Format matters. A sponsored Evertune analysis published on Search Engine Land found half of 25,000 heavily cited URLs were listicles, drawing 63% of citations. It also warns that Google is cracking down on self-promotional "best of" lists, and FTC rules bar a business from presenting its own site as an independent review of a category that includes itself.
  • The basics still apply. Google says normal SEO best practices remain relevant for its AI features.

What a local business should do

Skip the jargon and do the shared groundwork: claimed listings, consistent facts, reviews on several sites, crawlable pages that answer customer questions, and presence in structured sources agents read. See how to advertise to AI.

Frequently asked questions

Is GEO different from SEO?

It overlaps heavily. GEO adds focus on being mentioned and cited in AI answers, which rewards consistent facts and mentions across many sources, not just rankings and links.

What is the difference between GEO and AEO?

Mostly naming. AEO began with featured snippets and voice answers; GEO was coined for generative AI. Today both describe optimizing to be quoted in AI answers.

Does a small business need a GEO agency?

Not necessarily. The highest-impact steps (listings, reviews, clear website facts, crawler access) can be done in-house in a few hours.

What does AI SEO mean?

It can mean optimizing for AI answers, or using AI tools to do SEO. Ask a vendor which they mean.

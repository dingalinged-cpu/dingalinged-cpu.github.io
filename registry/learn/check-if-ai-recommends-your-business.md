# How to check whether AI recommends your business (free method)

*By Bryce Hoemann · Published October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** Write down ten questions customers actually ask, such as "plumber in Omaha" or "best dentist near 64111". Ask each one in ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity, logged out where possible, and note whether you are named and which sources are cited. Repeat weekly, because AI answers change between runs.

## The free method

1. **Pick ten prompts** in the shape customers use: "[service] in [city]", "[service] near [ZIP]", "best [service] in [city]", plus two or three specific needs ("emergency", "open Sunday", "takes insurance").
2. **Run each prompt in four assistants**: ChatGPT, Gemini (or Google AI Mode), Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity.
3. **Log four things** in a spreadsheet: date, assistant, whether you were named, and which sources were cited.
4. **Repeat weekly** for at least a month.

## Why one test is not enough

BrightLocal reran the same localized prompts and found only 20% to 33% of businesses overlapped between runs; a business that appeared once came back about half the time. A single screenshot proves little either way.

## Read the citations

The cited sources tell you where to work. If every answer cites the same two directories and you aren't on them, that is your next task.

## Paid tools

Tracking tools exist from Ahrefs, BrightLocal, Semrush and others. Search demand for terms like "AI brand mentions" (about 1,100 a month) and "AI citation tracking" (about 500) has appeared only since mid-2025, per Ahrefs. A manual log is enough for most single-location businesses.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a free way to see if ChatGPT recommends my business?

Yes. Ask ChatGPT the questions your customers ask, a few times a week, and log whether you are named and what sources are cited.

### Why do I appear in ChatGPT one day and not the next?

AI answers vary between runs. Studies found only 20% to 33% of recommended businesses reappeared when the same prompt was repeated.

### Do I need an AI visibility tool?

Not for one location. A weekly manual log of ten prompts across four assistants gives a useful picture. Tools help at scale.

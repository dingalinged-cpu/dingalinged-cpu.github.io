How to get your business into Microsoft Copilot answers (Bing Places, Bing Webmaster Tools, IndexNow)

Updated October 18, 2026

Short answer: Microsoft Copilot answers from Bing search results, so start with Bing. Claim your free Bing Places for Business listing, verify your website in Bing Webmaster Tools, and use IndexNow so Bing hears about new or changed pages quickly. Ads in Copilot are sold separately through Microsoft Advertising and labeled.

Why Bing matters more than you think

Microsoft says Copilot generates a short search query from your prompt, sends it to Bing, and grounds its response in the results; Copilot Search "is grounded on Bing search results." Bing Places for Business is Microsoft's free listing tool for Bing and Bing Maps, and Microsoft describes deeper integration with Copilot. Most local businesses claim Google and skip Bing, which leaves room.

Steps

  1. Claim Bing Places for Business. Add hours, photos, website, services and social links; Bing's recommendation tool suggests missing details that drive engagement.
  2. Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit your sitemap.
  3. Turn on IndexNow. It's a free protocol that tells Bing (and other participating engines) when a URL is added or changed. Bing notes submission does not guarantee immediate indexing.
  4. Keep facts consistent. Copilot cross-references sources.

What IndexNow does not do

IndexNow helps Bing find changes. There is no first-party statement that it improves visibility in ChatGPT; treat claims otherwise with caution.

Copilot ads

Microsoft says Search ads (via AI Max) can run on Copilot Search and Copilot Answers, and in April 2026 it introduced "Offer Highlights," an ad format inside Copilot conversations, for retail advertisers. Ads are labeled and separate from the answer.

Frequently asked questions

Does Microsoft Copilot use Bing Places?

Copilot grounds answers in Bing search, and Bing Places supplies business listings to Bing and Bing Maps. Claiming it is the most direct free step for Copilot.

Is Bing Places free?

Yes. Claiming and managing a Bing Places for Business listing is free.

Does IndexNow help with ChatGPT?

Not confirmed. IndexNow notifies Bing and other participating engines of changes; no first-party OpenAI statement says it affects ChatGPT.

Can I advertise in Copilot?

Yes, through Microsoft Advertising. Ads appear in Copilot experiences and are labeled separately from answers.

ChatGPT ads for a local business: how they work and when they are worth it (2026)

Updated October 18, 2026

Short answer: ChatGPT ads are labeled sponsored placements shown to logged-in adults on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans. Businesses buy them at ads.openai.com, now self-serve. They appear beside the answer and, per OpenAI, never change it. A local business should fix its listings and reviews first, since most AI recommendations are earned, not bought.

The timeline

  • January 16, 2026: OpenAI publishes its advertising principles.
  • February 9, 2026: ads test begins in the US for logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education plans have no ads.
  • May 5, 2026: OpenAI begins rolling out a beta self-serve Ads Manager for businesses of all sizes, adds cost-per-click (CPC) bidding next to the original cost-per-thousand-impressions (CPM) buying, and works with agency and technology partners.
  • August 2026: expansion to the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, South Korea and 31 European countries.
  • September 16, 2026: OpenAI begins testing "Sponsored Agents" with select US advertisers: after clicking an ad, a user can choose a clearly labeled conversation with a business-sponsored agent, separate from ChatGPT's own answers.

How the ads are chosen

OpenAI says it matches ads to the topic of the conversation, the user's past chats and past ad interactions. Ads are not shown to users believed to be under 18 or near sensitive topics such as health, mental health or politics. Advertisers get only aggregate numbers like views and clicks, never the chats.

Should a local business buy them?

Ask three questions first:

  1. Do I show up in the answer already? Ask ChatGPT the way customers do ("plumber in Omaha", "best dentist near 64111") a few times. If you never appear, fix the free signals first. See how to get recommended by ChatGPT.
  2. Is my category allowed? Health and other sensitive topics are excluded from ads.
  3. Can I measure it? OpenAI offers pixel-based measurement and a Conversions API, and you pay per click under CPC bidding. Without tracking, you will not know if a click became a customer.

What we will not tell you

We do not have independent cost or results data for ChatGPT ads, and we will not guess. If you see a cost figure, check its date and source; pricing has changed since launch.

Frequently asked questions

Who sees ChatGPT ads?

Logged-in adults on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans in markets where ads are live. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education plans do not show ads.

Where do I sign up to advertise on ChatGPT?

At ads.openai.com, OpenAI's advertiser site. OpenAI began rolling out a beta self-serve Ads Manager on May 5, 2026, and is opening it to more businesses gradually.

Do ChatGPT ads change which businesses ChatGPT recommends?

No, according to OpenAI. Ads are labeled and shown separately from the answer, and OpenAI says ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives.

Are ChatGPT ads available for health businesses?

OpenAI says ads are not eligible to appear near sensitive or regulated topics such as health, mental health or politics.

What should a local business do before buying ChatGPT ads?

Make sure your listings are accurate and consistent, you have recent reviews on several sites, and your website states your services, area and prices clearly. Those earn recommendations for free.

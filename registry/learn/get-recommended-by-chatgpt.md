# How to get your business recommended by ChatGPT: a free, honest checklist

*Updated October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** Make your business easy for ChatGPT to find, read and trust. Allow OpenAI's crawlers, keep your name, address, phone and hours identical on every listing, collect reviews on several sites, and state your services, area and prices plainly on your website. There is no submit button and no paid shortcut to the answer.

## How people ask ChatGPT for businesses

They type like they search. Sagapixel watched real ChatGPT sessions for local services and found that 75% included at least one keyword-style prompt, like "dentist 11214" or "dermatologist in seattle wa." So the questions to win are simple: "[service] in [city]," "[service] near me," "best [service] in [city]."

## The checklist

**1. Let OpenAI's crawlers in.** Your robots.txt should not block OAI-SearchBot (search), ChatGPT-User (pages users ask about) or GPTBot. A page ChatGPT cannot fetch cannot be cited.

**2. Fix your listings.** Claim Google Business Profile and Bing Places. Then check the big directories in your category. Use the exact same name, address, phone, hours and service list everywhere.

**3. Get reviews beyond Google.** BrightLocal's CEO notes that ChatGPT and other assistants "can't see inside Google's walled garden of reviews." In BrightLocal's study of 1.9 million local AI citations, Yelp was ChatGPT's most-used local source, appearing in 80% of its answers.

**4. Put facts on your website, in sentences.**
- What you do, and what you don't.
- The towns or ZIP codes you serve.
- Typical prices or price ranges.
- Hours, licenses, years in business.
- Answers to the five questions customers ask on the phone.

**5. Keep pages fresh.** Ahrefs' analysis of 17 million AI citations found AI-cited content was on average 25.7% fresher than pages ranking in Google's organic results. Update the date only when you update the content.

**6. Add structured data.** LocalBusiness schema markup won't win on its own (an Ahrefs test found schema markup didn't move AI citations), but it removes ambiguity about who you are.

**7. Show up in agent-readable sources.** Registries and directories that publish clean, structured business facts give ChatGPT one more consistent place to confirm you.

## Why you might still not appear

AI answers name only a few businesses and change between runs. BrightLocal found a median of 2 to 4 businesses per answer and only 20% to 33% overlap when a prompt was rerun. Measure over weeks, not one try.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a way to submit my business to ChatGPT?

No. There is no submission form for ChatGPT's answers. It finds businesses through its search partners and the public web, so the work is making your information accurate and easy to find.

### How long does it take to get recommended by ChatGPT?

There is no fixed timeline. Listing and website changes need to be recrawled, and reviews accumulate over time. Track it over several weeks with the same prompts.

### Does schema markup help with ChatGPT?

It helps machines read your facts without guessing, but studies show it is not a strong predictor on its own. Accurate listings, reviews and clear website text matter more.

### Why does ChatGPT recommend my competitor and not me?

Usually because the competitor has more reviews, more consistent listings, a higher local search rank, or a website that states its services and area more clearly.

### Which crawlers should I allow for ChatGPT?

OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search, ChatGPT-User for pages users ask ChatGPT to open, and GPTBot if you are comfortable with your content being used for training.

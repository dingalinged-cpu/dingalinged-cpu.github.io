# How to advertise to AI: every way a business can reach ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and AI agents (2026)

*Updated October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** You can reach customers through AI in two ways. Pay for labeled ads where an assistant sells them (ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Google's AI results). Or earn recommendations, which no one can buy: keep your listings accurate everywhere, collect reviews, publish clear facts on your website, and put them in agent-readable sources.

## Paid and earned are two different things

AI assistants now show two kinds of business mentions, and it matters which one you are after.

- **Paid placements** are ads. They are labeled "Sponsored" and sit next to the answer, not inside it. OpenAI says ads in ChatGPT "do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you."
- **Earned recommendations** are the businesses the assistant names in its answer. Nobody sells those. They come from what the assistant can find and trust about you on the web.

Most small businesses should start with earned recommendations. They cost nothing but time, and every assistant reads from the same public sources.

## How to earn AI recommendations (free)

Assistants recommend a short list. BrightLocal's study of 200,085 local prompts found a median of 2 to 4 businesses per answer. To be one of them, make your business easy to find, easy to read and easy to trust.

1. **Claim your main listings.** Google Business Profile feeds Google's AI: BrightLocal found Google used it for virtually every location it checked. Bing Places feeds Bing, which grounds Microsoft Copilot. For ChatGPT, BrightLocal found Yelp was the most-used local source, appearing in 80% of answers.
2. **Keep your name, address, phone, hours and services identical everywhere.** Assistants cross-check sources. Conflicting facts make you a riskier pick.
3. **Earn reviews on more than one site.** BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 45% of US consumers used AI tools for local business recommendations, and most of them double-check reviews before they choose.
4. **Say plainly on your website what you do, where, for whom and what it costs.** Assistants read text. A page that answers "Do you serve Plano?" or "How much is a drain cleaning?" in one sentence is easy to quote.
5. **Let AI crawlers in.** Check that your robots.txt does not block GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot or Bingbot.
6. **Get listed where agents read structured data.** Directories and registries that publish clean, machine-readable facts give assistants another consistent source to confirm you.
7. **Get mentioned.** Ahrefs' study of 75,000 brands found branded web mentions had the strongest correlation (0.664) with AI Overview visibility, stronger than backlinks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I pay ChatGPT to recommend my business?

No. You can buy ads in ChatGPT, which appear labeled as sponsored next to the answer, but OpenAI says ads do not influence what ChatGPT recommends. Recommendations come from what ChatGPT can find and trust about you on the web.

### What is the cheapest way to advertise to AI?

Earning recommendations is free: claim your Google Business Profile and Bing Places listing, keep your details identical everywhere, collect reviews, state your services and prices clearly on your website, and allow AI crawlers in robots.txt.

### Which AI assistants sell ads?

As of October 2026, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot sell ads, and Google has begun testing ads in AI Mode. Perplexity and Anthropic's Claude were reported not to be selling ads.

### How do AI assistants decide which local businesses to recommend?

Studies point to local search rank, review volume, presence on the directories each assistant reads, and the business's own website. Accurate, consistent facts across sources matter more than any single trick.

### Is advertising to AI different from SEO?

Partly. The groundwork is the same: be crawlable, accurate and well reviewed. The difference is that AI names only two to four businesses per answer, so consistency and trust across many sources carry more weight than one ranking.

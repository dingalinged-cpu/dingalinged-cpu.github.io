Bridging agentic and business marketing

Agentic commerce for small and local businesses: what it is and how to get ready

By Bryce Hoemann · Published October 18, 2026

Short answer: Agentic commerce is AI agents finding, comparing and sometimes buying on a customer's behalf. For a local or service business, the first step is not a checkout integration. It is being legible to agents: accurate, structured facts about services, prices, hours and area, published where agents read, plus a clear way to book or call.

What is happening

  • Demand: Ahrefs estimates "agentic commerce" at about 5,600 US searches a month, up 464% year over year (June 2026).
  • Standards: Stripe and OpenAI developed the Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP). Shopify and Google co-developed the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), which Shopify says is backed by Amazon, Microsoft, Visa, Mastercard, Walmart and others. The Model Context Protocol (MCP) lets agents call a business's tools and data directly.
  • Platforms: Stripe offers an Agentic Commerce Suite; Shopify connects merchants to AI shopping channels.

Stripe puts it simply: agents "need fast API responses, unambiguous policies and clean, structured data."

What it means for a local service business

Most agent activity for local services today is research: "find me three licensed electricians in Plano who can come this week." Before an agent can book you, it has to:

  1. Find you: listings, directories, registries.
  2. Understand you: services, service area, prices or ranges, hours, licenses, in plain text and structured data.
  3. Trust you: consistent facts, reviews.
  4. Act: a booking link, a phone number, or an online form it can hand to the customer.

Free first steps

  • Publish services, service area and price ranges as plain text on your site.
  • Keep hours and phone current on every listing.
  • Add a direct booking link where you can.
  • Claim profiles in sources that publish your facts in machine-readable form.

Checkout integrations (ACP, UCP) matter most for online retailers. Most local service businesses can wait.

Frequently asked questions

What is agentic commerce?

AI agents finding, comparing and sometimes buying products or services on a person's behalf, often with the person approving the final step.

Do local service businesses need ACP or UCP?

Not yet for most. Those protocols cover checkout for online sellers. Service businesses benefit first from clear, structured, consistent information and an easy way to book.

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI agents call a business's tools and data, such as inventory, pricing or availability, in a structured way.

How do I make my business agent-ready for free?

Publish your services, area, prices, hours and booking options in plain text, keep listings consistent, and claim profiles in machine-readable directories.

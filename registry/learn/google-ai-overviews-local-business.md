# How a local business gets into Google AI Overviews and AI Mode

*By Bryce Hoemann · Published October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** Google says there is no special setup for AI Overviews or AI Mode. Your page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet, and normal SEO applies. For local businesses, a complete Google Business Profile, steady reviews and pages that directly answer customer questions are the strongest levers.

## What Google officially says

From Google Search Central: to be eligible as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode, "a page must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet." There are "no additional technical requirements." AI Overviews show only when Google decides they add something to the results.

Google's AI optimization guide (May 2026) also says you don't need special machine-readable files such as llms.txt.

## Why it matters for local

A Whitespark study cited by Search Engine Journal found AI Overviews appeared on 68% of local business-type searches, against 39% for the local pack. Cost questions such as "average cost of dental implants in Phoenix" triggered them 97% of the time. AI Mode can now also check local availability and pricing, and Google says it can even call businesses for details that aren't online.

## Checklist

1. **Complete Google Business Profile**: categories, services, hours, photos, attributes. BrightLocal found Google used Business Profile for virtually every location it checked.
2. **Reviews that mention services and places** in customers' own words.
3. **Pages that answer one question each**: "How much does a water heater replacement cost in Kansas City?" Answer it in the first sentence.
4. **Snippet-eligible pages**: no `nosnippet`, indexable, fast.
5. **Prices and availability online**, so AI Mode does not need to call you to get them.

## Sponsored results

Google began testing ads in AI Mode in October 2026. Ads are separate from the organic supporting links.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need special markup to appear in AI Overviews?

No. Google says there are no additional technical requirements beyond being indexed and eligible to show a snippet.

### Does Google use llms.txt for AI Overviews?

Google has said its AI features do not use or need llms.txt.

### How important is Google Business Profile for AI Overviews?

Very. BrightLocal found Google's AI used Business Profile data for virtually every local business it described.

### Can AI Mode call my business?

Google says AI Mode can gather information by calling businesses when details are not available online. Publishing prices and availability reduces the need.

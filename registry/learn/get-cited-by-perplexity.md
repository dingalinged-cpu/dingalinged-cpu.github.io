# How to get your business cited by Perplexity

*By Bryce Hoemann · Published October 18, 2026*

**Short answer:** Perplexity has no form for adding a business. It searches the web live and cites pages it can fetch and trust. Allow PerplexityBot in robots.txt, answer common customer questions in the first sentence of a page, keep listings consistent, and earn genuine mentions in community discussions and local press that Perplexity often cites.

## What we know about Perplexity's sources

- Profound's analysis of 680 million citations (August 2024 to June 2025) found Reddit was Perplexity's most-cited source (6.6% of all its citations), followed by YouTube (2.0%).
- Local review sites also showed up in Perplexity's top ten: Yelp (0.8%) and Tripadvisor (0.6%).
- Perplexity was reported to have stopped selling ads in February 2026, so citations are the only route.

## Steps

1. **Be fetchable.** Allow PerplexityBot (and Perplexity-User) in robots.txt. A page it cannot fetch cannot be cited.
2. **Lead with the answer.** If the question is "Does [your business] do emergency repairs on weekends?", the first sentence on the page should say yes or no.
3. **Keep listings consistent** across Google, Bing, Apple and category directories.
4. **Be part of real conversations.** Answer questions honestly in local forums and community threads where you have something useful to add; do not astroturf. Communities remove promotional posts, and fake reviews violate consumer protection rules.
5. **Publish helpful video** if you can: short how-to clips about your service.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I list my business on Perplexity?

There is no public listing form. Perplexity finds businesses through live web search, so the work is being fetchable, accurate and mentioned in sources it trusts.

### Does Perplexity sell ads?

Perplexity was reported to have stopped selling ads in February 2026. Check Perplexity's own announcements for the latest.

### Why does Perplexity cite Reddit so much?

Citation studies show Perplexity leans on community discussion. Genuine, helpful participation can help; promotional posting usually backfires.
